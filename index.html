GDP Research Dashboard

An independent macroeconomic research dashboard for exploring the size and direction of the global economy. A few working principles

Good analysis should be traceable.

Good financial software needs more than a clean interface. The sources, assumptions and calculations have to stand up too.

I want the numbers to be traceable, the calculations to be explainable and the product to feel straightforward when someone sits down to use it.

That is the standard I try to bring to every project, whether I’m working with market data, building a research tool or putting together a website for a business.

Sources first

Know where the numbers came from.

I keep source dates and gaps visible. A fresh import is not automatically a fresh observation, and missing values should stay missing.

Clear assumptions

Show what the model is doing.

Calculated values and scenarios should be distinguishable from published observations. I try not to hide a model’s limitations behind a polished chart.

Useful products

Make the next step obvious.

I care about whether someone can find the information they need and use the result without fighting the interface.

For me, a good result is not the most complicated thing I can build. It is something that helps someone understand an issue more clearly, save a little time or make a better-informed decision.
