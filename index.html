A little about me

Curious by discipline.
Practical by design.

I enjoy work that needs both analytical thinking and careful execution — from understanding financial markets to building the software that makes information easier to use.

Paul NdwekiaGitHub photo is the defaultChange profile photo

I study Financial Engineering at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology. Alongside my studies, I build software projects that explore the relationship between financial systems, data and everyday decisions.

My work is grounded in the Kenyan context, with a particular interest in capital markets, fixed income, foreign exchange, quantitative methods and useful digital products.

I value clear assumptions, honest project status and interfaces that feel considered rather than complicated.
